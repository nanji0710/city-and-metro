including waterways, roads, railways, green areas, boundaries and place names.
This covers the four upstream scenes (geo_gz Guangzhou, geo_hz Hangzhou,
geo_hk Hong Kong, geo_tk Tokyo) plus the 54 city scenes added by this fork
(js/geo_<city>.js, city ids listed in js/data_cities.js).
